Each subpath's modules now have their collection's license beside them, as `generated/<subpath>/LICENSE`. A tool that takes a file's license from the nearest license file, as Soundor's builds do for the third-party notices they ship, now gives the icons Lucide's, Tabler's, Phosphor's, Heroicons' or Material Symbols' license instead of the package's MIT.
